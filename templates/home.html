About IGEC

Building Tomorrow's Engineers

Indira Gandhi Engineering College, Sagar was established in 1996 by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. Located on NH-26, Sagar–Damoh Road, the campus is spread across a lush green landscape and is affiliated to Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya (RGPV), Bhopal.

With well-equipped laboratories, a central library, experienced faculty, and a strong emphasis on practical learning, the college has consistently produced skilled engineers who serve across industries and government sectors.
