Discover IGEC Sagar

A premier government autonomous engineering institution in Madhya Pradesh, empowering technical minds since 1996.

Our Heritage

Established in 1996, Indira Gandhi Engineering College (IGEC) is a highly respected government-autonomous institute located in Sagar, Madhya Pradesh.

Spread across a lush 220-acre campus, IGEC has continually fostered a culture of innovation, hands-on learning, and technical excellence. Our mission is to produce highly skilled engineers who are ready to face global challenges and contribute meaningfully to society.

Academic Excellence & Facilities

We provide top-notch education and comprehensive facilities to ensure our students thrive academically and personally.

Undergraduate Courses

We offer B.E. (Bachelor of Engineering) in multiple disciplines through state-level counseling:

  • Computer Engineering / IT
  • Civil Engineering
  • Electrical Engineering
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Electronics & Communication
  • Chemical Engineering

Campus Facilities

A 220-acre vibrant campus equipped with modern amenities:

  • Separate Boys & Girls Hostels
  • Well-equipped Labs & Workshops
  • Central Library with internet
  • Sports & Recreation grounds
  • Auditorium & Seminar Halls
  • Cafeteria & Medical Dispensary

Career & Growth

Our dedicated Training and Placement Cell actively works with leading tech companies and core industries to provide excellent career opportunities for our students.

Beyond academics, students engage in technical fests, robotics, coding contests, and sports, ensuring a holistic development that prepares them for leadership roles in the industry.
